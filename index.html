Protein, spread across the day

Breakfast is a start, not the whole target.

Four eggs or 150 g of chicken in the morning covers a slice of the 140–160 g goal. The food log shows what's left and the quickest foods to close the gap. Whey stays optional.

What it keeps track of

Only numbers you logged. Nothing made up.

Progressive overload

When every set reaches the top of the rep range, it suggests a small jump. You accept, edit or ignore it — weights never change by themselves.

Streaks that respect rest

Thursdays and Fridays never break a streak. Rescheduled sessions move instead of counting as missed.

Food around 4:30 PM

Meal timing built around the gym slot, quick-add for eggs, chicken, dal and curd, and clearly labelled estimates.

Measurements & charts

Weight, waist, chest, arms and thighs with trend lines, plus strength charts for every exercise.

Private photos

Front, side and back photos in a private folder, with a before-and-after slider only you can open.

Survives bad signal

Sets queue on the phone when the connection drops and sync when it's back. "Saved" means the server confirmed it.
